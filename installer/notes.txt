cheesestrap, a small roblox client bootstrapper.

no official installer, the client is downloaded into our own clients/ folder
and updated before every launch. the interface is a ratatui tui painted inside
our own window, so there is no console behind it.

this build:

- versions are the build time, nothing to bump by hand
- the app updates itself from the github release, it offers the update and
  installs the setup for you
- webview2 is detected and repaired instead of the broken delete button that
  used to leave a stale registration behind
- data lives next to the exe: clients/, logs/, settings.json
